## Where I come from

I started in 2008 at Halliburton as an L/MWD field engineer, preparing telemetry tools for drilling, and later moved
into reliability, doing root cause analysis of failures. I stayed in the oil industry at NOV, building reports and
scripts in Power BI and C# for well completion and cementing.

Since then I’ve worked with data in very different sectors: operations and compliance at a **fintech** (Ugami),
neural-network models to improve sugarcane productivity in an **agritech and healthcare** project (Digisalud) and,
today, operational BI at **PengCorp**. That range is what I bring to AI engineering: real problems, imperfect data
and users who need answers they can trust.

## What I work on

- **LLMs and RAG.** Assistants that answer from your own documents, cite their sources and say "I don’t know" when they should.
- **Vector search.** Embeddings, ANN indexes and honest retrieval evaluation.
- **Conversational and voice assistants.** Multi-turn flows with real business rules.
- **Classical machine learning.** From exploratory analysis to a model served behind an API.

## How I work

I write the spec before the code, and I don’t consider a requirement done without executable evidence: a test,
a metric or a reproducible run. I prefer simple solutions that can be measured over impressive architectures
that can’t be explained.
